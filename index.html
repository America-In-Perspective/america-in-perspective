About this project

We study other countries to better understand our own.

America in Perspective is an evolving, independent educational resource. We compare measurable outcomes among prosperous democracies, investigate the different policies countries have chosen, and make sourced evidence easy to understand and share.

Our objective is not to challenge anyone's love of country or claim that one nation's choices are universally right. It is to ask what the data actually show, identify uncertainties, and explore what citizens and policymakers can learn from international experience.

Research principles

Every published statistic should identify its source, population, unit and reporting period. We distinguish outcomes from explanations, correlation from demonstrated causality, and objective measurements from judgments about trade-offs. We welcome corrections backed by reliable sources.
