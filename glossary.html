Glossary

Plain-language explanations of terms used throughout America in Perspective. In-article definitions also appear when you hover or tap highlighted words.


Age-standardized mortality
A death rate adjusted for differences in population ages.
OECD
Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development. An international group that collects comparable economic and social data.
Per capita
Per person.
PPP (purchasing power parity)
An adjustment that accounts for differences in purchasing power across countries.
Preventable mortality
Deaths that could mainly be avoided through public health and prevention measures, according to a defined list of causes.
Treatable mortality
Deaths that could mainly be avoided with timely, effective medical care, based on a standardized cause list.
Universal healthcare coverage
Access to a defined set of essential healthcare services across a population; not necessarily a single government insurance program.
Single-payer healthcare
An insurance arrangement where one main payer finances covered healthcare services.
Underinsured
Having insurance but facing out-of-pocket costs or deductibles sufficiently large relative to income under a specified study definition.
Uncompensated care
Care for which a hospital does not receive full payment; includes charity care and unpaid bills. Some public payments offset providers’ costs.
Cost shifting
An attempt by a provider to recover losses from one payer by charging another payer more; the extent varies and is debated.
DSH (Disproportionate Share Hospital)
A Medicaid payment program that supports certain hospitals serving many low-income or uninsured patients.
