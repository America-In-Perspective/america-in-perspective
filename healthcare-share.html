Short, attributed facts ready to paste into social media. The healthcare metric pages also let you download charts as PNG images.

Healthcare spending

The United States spent $14,885 per person on healthcare in 2024 or nearest year, compared with the OECD average of $5,967 (PPP-adjusted). Source: OECD, Health at a Glance 2025.

Preventable mortality

The U.S. age-standardized preventable mortality rate was 217 per 100,000 versus an OECD average of 145 (2023 or nearest). Source: OECD, Health at a Glance 2025.

Treatable mortality

The U.S. treatable mortality rate was 95 per 100,000 versus an OECD average of 77 (2023 or nearest). Source: OECD, Health at a Glance 2025.
