About America in Perspective

We study other countries not to diminish America, but to better understand it.

About this project

We study other countries to better understand our own.

America in Perspective is an evolving, independent educational resource. We compare measurable outcomes among prosperous democracies, investigate the different policies countries have chosen, and make sourced evidence easy to understand and share.

Our objective is not to challenge anyone's love of country or claim that one nation's choices are universally right. It is to ask what the data actually show, identify uncertainties, and explore what citizens and policymakers can learn from international experience.

Research principles

Every published statistic should identify its source, population, unit and reporting period. We distinguish outcomes from explanations, correlation from demonstrated causality, and objective measurements from judgments about trade-offs. We welcome corrections backed by reliable sources.

Research standards & sources

Each published finding should identify the original source, the data year or years, the population, units, and relevant limits of comparability. Wherever possible, we compare ten peer countries and the OECD average, and show trends over time. We distinguish measured outcomes from explanations of causes, and identify when an explanation is supported by evidence rather than established conclusively.

Our policy articles aim for a clear, approximately 500-word introduction; more complicated questions may require more explanation. Sources and methods remain available to readers who want additional detail.

Contact & corrections

We welcome documented corrections, missing sources, and suggestions for future subjects. A public project contact method is being prepared; until one is published, no personal contact details will be collected or displayed here. Once a correction process is in place, changes will identify the date of review and explain material revisions.
