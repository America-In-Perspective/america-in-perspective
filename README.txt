America in Perspective — Healthcare Library Prototype Version 3
November 22, 2026

GET STARTED
1. Extract this ZIP archive.
2. Open index.html in Edge, Safari, Chrome or Firefox.
3. Select 'Health library' in the navigation to see the research catalog.
4. Use the subject filters. On verified cards, try Copy finding, Copy data or Save chart.
5. Use the main Healthcare comparison for ten-country plus OECD-average life expectancy and spending views.

WHAT'S NEW
- Healthcare library organized into Health & longevity, Prevention & disease, Care effectiveness, Cost & access, and Health inequality.
- 17 research indicators including new requested metrics (maternal mortality, chronic disease, premature chronic disease mortality, vaccination, U.S. inequality, financial access, healthy life expectancy, mental health).
- Verified source-linked example findings for preventable mortality, treatable mortality, life expectancy, and health spending.
- Copy-ready source-attributed findings, tabular values, and chart PNG downloads for the verified cards.
- Clearly marked research-in-progress items, without invented national values or trend data.

EDITORIAL NOTE
- First-stage research prototype, not a final fully audited database. Indicator years and geographical definitions may differ.
- Existing 10-country life-expectancy and spending dataset is retained from Version 2 as reported in OECD Health at a Glance 2025. U.S. life expectancy in the 2025 report is 78.4 (2023); the newer CDC 2024 figure is 79.0. Do not mix years inadvertently.
- OECD averages represent all OECD countries, not the countries visible in selected charts.
- Source URLs need internet to open, but the site works offline.
- These international comparisons do not establish that any policy by itself caused the outcomes.
