Healthcare · Research library v3

Explore the outcomes. Investigate the policies.

Five groups of healthcare questions. Verified comparisons are ready to share; other measures are transparently marked Research in progress until cross-country figures and definitions have been checked.

Every completed finding will have: an international comparison, a readable table, a copyable plain-text version, a downloadable chart image, source links, a policy explainer, and historical trends when reliable comparable data are available. For U.S. health inequality, the emphasis will be on disparities within the country.

About fair comparisons: The OECD average includes all OECD members, not merely the ten countries shown on our other charts. Reporting years can differ by country. We never substitute unverified figures for missing data, and a correlation between a policy and an outcome is not proof of causation.
